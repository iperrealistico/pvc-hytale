What is Peaceful Vanilla Club in Hytale

Peaceful Vanilla Club is a peaceful vanilla survival community that has been running on Minecraft for years and now runs a shared semi-vanilla survival world for Hytale.

On Hytale, Peaceful Vanilla Club is a single shared survival world with no random PvP and no griefing. The focus is exploration, building, long term towns and cooperative projects rather than minigames or fast seasons.

There are no pay to win ranks, no overpowered donor kits and no aggressive monetization. We aim for a fair Hytale vanilla survival server vibe with a semi-vanilla approach that protects builders and community progress.

The community is inclusive and LGBTQ+ friendly. Moderation is strict against harassment, slurs and exploit abuse.

Our goals for a vanilla Hytale server

These are the core goals that guide how we run Peaceful Vanilla Club as a semi-vanilla Hytale survival server.

No random PvP

PvP is not enabled in the open survival world. If we host combat events, they are opt-in and clearly separated.

Stress free

No pay to win

We do not sell power. No overpowered donor kits, boosts or shortcuts that break the vanilla survival loop.

Fairness

Builder safe rules

No griefing, no stealing, and clear reporting. We protect community builds and long-term progress.

Hytale server tags and what they mean

People search for different things. Here is what we mean when we say semi-vanilla and peaceful survival.

Vanilla
Vanilla means the core survival loop stays intact. No heavy modpacks, no forced minigames, no drastic changes to progression.
Semi-vanilla
Semi-vanilla means small quality-of-life rules and protections, like anti-grief tools and moderation. The gameplay remains survival-first.
Peaceful
Peaceful means no random PvP, no harassment, and a builder-friendly culture. Cooperation over chaos.
