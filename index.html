What is Peaceful Vanilla Club in Hytale

Peaceful Vanilla Club is a peaceful vanilla survival community that has been running on Minecraft for years and is now preparing a shared survival world for Hytale.

On Hytale, Peaceful Vanilla Club will be a single shared survival world with no random PvP and no griefing. The focus is on exploration, building, long term towns and cooperative projects rather than minigames or fast seasons.

There are no pay to win ranks, no overpowered donor kits and no aggressive monetization. We keep the vanilla sandbox feeling intact and build a safe environment where players of all ages can relax and enjoy survival.

The community is inclusive and LGBTQ+ friendly. Moderation is strict against harassment, slurs and exploit abuse. Our goal is to offer a space that feels calm, cozy and reliable, whether you log in a few evenings per week or every day.

Our goals

These are the core goals that guide how we run Peaceful Vanilla Club on Hytale. They are the same values that kept our Minecraft survival worlds alive for years.

No random PvP

PvP is not enabled in the open survival world. If we host combat events, they are opt-in and clearly separated. For everyday play you can explore and build without stress.

Stress free

No pay to win

We do not sell power. No overpowered donor kits, boosts or shortcuts that break the vanilla survival loop. Any future donations will support hosting costs and cosmetic ideas that do not affect gameplay balance.

Fairness

No fast travel spam

There are no instant warps or random teleport commands that break the scale of the world. The world feels big and your builds feel grounded.

Exploration first

A peaceful place

We design the server so that you can log in, relax, make progress on your base or town and log out without drama or pressure. Chat, voice and events follow the same calm, respectful tone.

Mood

Community-first decisions

Changes and new features are introduced slowly and discussed with the community. Stability and fairness come before hype. We prefer fewer systems that work well over a long list of unfinished features.

Governance

Long term worlds

Our goal is to avoid routine world resets. When new Hytale content lands, we look for ways to integrate it without wiping everything, so that towns and landscapes can gain history over time.

Persistence

How to join the Hytale server

Joining Peaceful Vanilla Club in Hytale will be simple. You will not need an application or paywall, but you will need to join the Discord and read the rules before connecting.

  1. 1

    Join the Discord server

    Discord is where we coordinate everything. Join the Peaceful Vanilla Club Discord using the link below and verify your account if required. This is where we will post the Hytale launch date and server address.

  2. 2

    Read the full rules

    The complete rules will be published in a dedicated Discord channel. Reading them is required before playing. They explain how we handle griefing, chat behaviour, claims and community projects.

  3. 3

    Add the server when Hytale launches

    When Hytale servers open, you will find the server address, recommended client settings and any technical notes pinned in the announcements channel. Add the server in the Hytale multiplayer menu and join the world.

If you are already part of the Minecraft server, you will feel at home. If you are new to Peaceful Vanilla Club, the Discord is the best place to ask questions and understand how the Hytale world will work.

Basic rules that keep things peaceful

  • No griefing or stealing from other players.
  • No unsolicited PvP, traps or kill machines targeting others.
  • No hacks, exploits or automation that breaks the survival loop.
  • No harassment, slurs or toxic behaviour in chat or voice.
  • Respect public builds, towns and infrastructure projects.

The full rules are available in our Discord and you are required to read them before playing. The short version is simple: play fair, be kind and remember that you share the world with others.
