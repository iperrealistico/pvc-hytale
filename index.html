What is Peaceful Vanilla Club in Hytale

A cozy survival server for players who are tired of minigames, pay to win ranks and chaotic PvP. If you love slow exploration, building and community stories, this is for you.

Peaceful Vanilla Club is a Hytale vanilla survival server focused on long term, low stress gameplay. You spawn in a shared world that never resets and you write your own story with friends and strangers who play the same way.

There are no kits that break the game, no overpowered donor items and no random teleport spam. We keep the core Hytale sandbox experience intact and add only a few quality of life features that help with safety and social interaction.

The community is inclusive and LGBTQ+ friendly. Moderation is strict against harassment, toxicity and griefing. Our goal is to make you feel at home in a persistent world that rewards creativity and collaboration instead of speed and aggression.

Friendly and LGBTQ+ inclusive No pay to win or aggressive monetization Long term worlds without resets

Our principles

The design rules that shape how the Hytale server feels. These principles apply to every world we run, and they are what make Peaceful Vanilla Club different from most Hytale servers.

No fast travel spam

There are no instant warps or random teleport commands that break the scale of the world. Travel is done by walking, sailing, building roads and rails. The world feels big and your builds feel grounded.

Exploration first

No random PvP

PvP is not enabled in the open world. If you enjoy fighting you can join specific opt in events and arenas. The rest of the time you can build and explore without worrying about getting ambushed.

Stress free

A peaceful place

Survival can be challenging, but the social atmosphere is meant to be calm and friendly. You can log in after work or school, do a small project and log out without drama or pressure.

Mood

Quality of life

We keep the vanilla Hytale feeling, but add small quality of life tools that protect builds, simplify trading and make navigation easier. No features that trivialize the survival loop or progression.

Vanilla plus

Nostalgic feeling

The world generation, pacing and survival rules are tuned to feel closer to the golden age of sandbox survival than to modern fast content loops. Builds and landscapes are meant to last and be remembered.

Vibes

No world resets

We do not plan regular world resets. Your base, your town and your landmarks are meant to stay. We handle new content and updates with care, preserving as much history as possible.

Persistence

Core features for Hytale players

These systems make the Hytale vanilla survival experience safer, more social and more interesting without breaking the core sandbox gameplay loop.

Land claims and protection

Secure your base and builds with a simple land claiming system. Claim only what you need and expand over time. This protects against griefing while still allowing organic towns and shared projects.

No random protected chunks that break the map. Claims are visible, fair and tuned for long term survival.

Player driven economy

Set up shops, trade resources and run services. The economy is entirely player driven, with no pay to win shortcuts or admin shops that flood the market. Prices and meta evolve naturally.

Town markets, trading halls and small personal shops are all welcome as long as they respect the peaceful nature of the server.

Playtime ranks

The more you play, the more you unlock small perks like extra set homes, cosmetic titles or access to special community areas. No gameplay advantages tied to real money, only to time and trust.

Ranks act as a measure of trust and commitment, not as a paywall. New players can enjoy the full survival experience from day one.

How to join the Hytale server

Joining Peaceful Vanilla Club in Hytale is simple. You do not need a rank, an invite or an application. You only need the official game, a stable internet connection and respect for a few basic rules.

  1. 1

    Install and update Hytale

    Download Hytale from the official launcher on your PC and keep it updated. Peaceful Vanilla Club always targets a stable and widely used version. When major updates happen we announce support in advance on Discord.

  2. 2

    Add the server to your multiplayer list

    Open the multiplayer section from the Hytale main menu. Add a new server using the address hytale.peacefulvanilla.club. Give it a custom name if you like, for example "Peaceful Vanilla Club".

  3. 3

    Read the rules and spawn safely

    When you join for the first time you spawn in a safe hub. Take a moment to read the rules on the signs or via the in game guide. From there you can walk out into the world and start your survival journey.

If you ever feel lost you can ask for help in the Discord server. Staff and long term players are used to guiding new Hytale players through their first steps on PVC.

Basic rules that keep things peaceful

  • No griefing or stealing from other players.
  • No unsolicited PvP, traps or kill machines targeting others.
  • No hacks, exploits or automation that breaks the survival loop.
  • No harassment, slurs or toxic behaviour in chat or voice.
  • Respect public builds, towns and infrastructure projects.

The full rules are available in game and in the Discord. The short version is simple: play fair, be kind and remember that you share the world with others.
